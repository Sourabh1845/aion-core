Emergent Ventures call brief

AION Core: trust infrastructure for AI actions.

AION started as a trust/control layer for AI agents. It sits between AI systems and real-world tools, deciding what can run, what needs approval, what must be blocked, and what evidence should be recorded.

The Clean Story

I applied with AION as AI agent trust/security infrastructure. Since applying, I built the first open-source core MVP across 8 stages: Guard, Receipts, Scan, Docs/Demo, Cloud alignment, MCP Firewall, Team Policy, and Control Panel.

LaunchShield and VibeOps are not pivots away from the original idea. They are applied proof surfaces. LaunchShield scans AI workflows before launch. VibeOps shows how AI can run small-business operations with approval, memory, and evidence.

What Exists

  • PyPI package: aion-core
  • one-command demo
  • MCP firewall
  • receipt verification
  • scan CLI
  • team approval decisions
  • LangChain, CrewAI, Groq, and MCP proof tests
  • single-agent and multi-agent capacity tests

Why Now

AI is moving from chat into real actions: files, tools, customer messages, databases, payments, CRMs, and operations. That creates a missing infrastructure layer for permissions, approvals, evidence, and operator visibility.

How To Explain VibeOps

Owner command -> AION parses intent -> approval -> job/payment/receipt/report

VibeOps is a distribution wedge and applied proof environment. It shows the same AION thesis in a concrete small-business workflow: AI can act, but the owner remains in control.
